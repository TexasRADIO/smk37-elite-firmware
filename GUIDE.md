# BB–37 Telegram 101

Build 852 · Player guide

Telegram 101 puts a 101 lead, Dillo acid bass and an 808 drum machine on the M-VAVE SMK-37 Elite, SMK-37 Pro and MKE-P37.

[Install BB–37 Telegram 101](https://thebigbeat.app/elite/telegram-101/install/) · [Download Build 852](https://github.com/TexasRADIO/smk37-elite-firmware/releases/tag/build-852) · [Support](https://github.com/TexasRADIO/smk37-elite-firmware/issues) · [Return to M-VAVE firmware](https://thebigbeat.app/elite/install/#return)

## Install by USB

Back up your work. Connect one powered instrument directly with a USB data cable. Close other apps using its MIDI port. In desktop Chrome or Edge, open the installer, click Install BB–37 and allow MIDI access. Keep the cable connected until the page confirms completion. The downloadable ZIP includes the same installer for offline use.

## Play your first sound

On Telegram Home, play a key. On the first knob page, turn K7 (SOUND) to step through sounds or K8 (BANK) to move between banks. If you have assigned those knobs yourself, your assignments take priority. Use the pads to select sounds within a bank.

## Shape a sound

Hold a note and turn a sound control. On the filter card, NOW shows the cutoff while the voice sounds. On the envelope card, release the key to see the release stage.

## Record a knob for one bar

On a Telegram playing page, turn a sound knob until its card reads REC: RECORD A BAR. Press REC while the card is visible, then move that knob. Your first movement starts the recording. After one bar it loops. Turning the same knob shifts the gesture around its new setting. To clear the loop, bring that knob’s card back and press REC. One gesture plays at a time. It follows pattern changes and clears when you load another sound or power off.

## Play the Band

On Band, press PATCH to browse the Library shelves. On SONGS, select a song with K1 and tap a lit pad to cue a part. During playback all three parts change together on the next bar. SEQ PLAY starts and stops the groove. BT leaves a shelf. The Band screen shows which synth the keys play.

## Percussion and backups

All three models include the synthesized 808. On Elite, the installer also checks the 727 and LATIN kits. If it prepares a percussion backup, download it and keep it for that keyboard. Use the installer’s restore control before returning to factory firmware if you want to restore the original percussion area.

## Interrupted install and return

If USB is interrupted in update mode, reconnect the same keyboard and use Install again. For factory firmware, use the return control on the BB–37 install page with M-VAVE’s version 016 file for your exact model.
