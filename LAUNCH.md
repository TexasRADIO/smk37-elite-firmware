**Big Beat Studio releases Telegram 101 for SMK-37 and FM-1**

**The 101. The 303. An 808. The band fits in the box.**

Telegram 101 brings Big Beat Studio's lead synth, acid bass and drum machine together in free firmware for the M-VAVE SMK-37 Elite, SMK-37 Pro, MKE-P37 and FM-1.

Telegram is our take on the SH-101: saw, pulse, sub and noise through a four-pole filter, with pulse-width modulation, glide, an arpeggiator and a sequencer. The sound bank includes the forty patches charted in Roland's SH-101 owner's manual. Flute to Thunder, with plenty of room to turn the knobs yourself.

Dillo is our take on the TB-303, with saw and square, a resonant filter, accent and slide. A synthesized 808 gives both instruments something to play against. Band cues the parts together instead of leaving three machines arguing about where the next bar starts.

Record one bar of a sound-knob movement and let it loop while you play. The screen follows the filter and envelope as the voice sounds. On Elite, the release also includes 727 and LATIN percussion; Pro, MKE-P37 and FM-1 retain the synthesized 808.

**BB–37 Telegram 101 is Build 851. Telegram 101 for FM-1 is Build 857.** Each has its own browser installer, player guide, public source and release download. The SMK-37 installer selects the exact package for the connected model. The FM-1 installer serves the FM-1 package. No Terminal or separate flashing utility.
