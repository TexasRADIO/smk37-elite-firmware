# BB–37 Telegram 101 · Build 856

Telegram 101 opens when you power on the keyboard. Tap BT on a playing page, then tap top-row pad 1 for Telegram, 2 for Dillo, 3 for 808 or 4 for Band. On Elite with its percussion kit loaded, pad 5 opens the 727. The active parts light up in the picker.

The Telegram Home display keeps the patch and bank visible as you play. The pitch readout has its own space above the knobs.

On Elite, the Band page gives the 727 its own fourth row. Turn K4 clockwise from OFF to select HANAN WHITE. During playback the percussion enters on the next downbeat. Selecting the next rhythm lets the current one finish the bar. Turn back past the first rhythm to mute immediately.

Elite includes the 727 and LATIN percussion banks. SMK-37 Pro and MKE-P37 include Telegram, Dillo, the synthesized 808 and Band.
